Interactive Booking & Constraint Simulator

GiST Conflict Protected

Test the PostgreSQL EXCLUDE USING gist rule directly. If you book an overlapping time slot for a doctor or patient who already has an active appointment, PostgreSQL will reject the transaction at the storage layer!

How Overlap Prevention Works

1

tstzrange with Half-Open [)

Consecutive appointments (10:00-10:30 and 10:30-11:00) meet at the boundary without colliding because the end point is excluded.

2

Partial GiST Index Filter

Exclusion is scoped to WHERE (status IN ('scheduled', 'confirmed', 'checked_in')). If cancelled or completed, the slot is immediately liberated.

3

ACID Concurrency Safety

Zero race conditions even under 100 simultaneous concurrent booking threads at the exact same millisecond.
